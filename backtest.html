Engine Scorecards
How to read: a well-calibrated engine has ~90% of actual outcomes inside its P5–P95 band, ~50% inside P25–P75, and actuals above the median ~50% of the time. Under-coverage of the 90% band means the cone is too narrow (overconfident) — GBM typically shows this because real returns have fatter tails than the log-normal assumption; Bootstrap and Factor usually cover better.
🔧 Auto-Calibrationconformal-style band correction learned from this backtest
Adjusted levels are the empirical quantiles of where actual outcomes landed inside each engine's simulated distribution (PIT). Applying stores them in this browser; the Steam Gauge Prediction tab picks them up automatically and re-labels its bands. What this fixes: band width and drift bias as measured over this sample. What it cannot fix: the future differing from the last decade — a calibration learned on a bull era bakes that era in. Re-run and re-apply periodically; clear it if the regime shifts.
